    How records are counted

    Input, output, cache reads and cache writes stay separate. Magpie estimates the API-equivalent cost; Amp credits are not converted to dollars.

    Records must have a reliable timestamp and stay unchanged across scans before import. Missing dates and changed records are reported here instead of being silently reassigned or added again.

    Known gateway matches are excluded. If you route Amp through Magpie and the gateway cannot identify those requests, exclude the affected threads or models in this plugin's options before enabling collection.

    Pausing stops future imports. It does not remove records already written to Magpie.
