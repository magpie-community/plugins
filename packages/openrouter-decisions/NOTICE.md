The OpenCode 2.x compatibility bridge, `opencode2.mjs`, is an unmodified
copy of `scripts/opencode2.mjs` from magpie-community/plugins, distributed
under that repository's MIT license. Keep it byte-identical to that copy.

Upstream: https://github.com/magpie-community/plugins

The implementation follows magpie's public provider/auth/decision contracts
and OpenRouter's public Decisions API. No third-party provider application
logic is bundled here.
