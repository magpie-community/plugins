# Deepzora icon

The PNG embedded in `package.json` (`magpie.icon`) and `deepzora.agent.js`
(`agent.icon`) is the unmodified official Deepzora 128 × 128 application icon.

Source: [SivanCola/deepzora at ad855397cdfdaf2ba7e3e3414583ba777412cbe4](https://github.com/SivanCola/deepzora/blob/ad855397cdfdaf2ba7e3e3414583ba777412cbe4/desktop/build/linux/icons/hicolor/128x128/apps/deepzora-desktop.png).
