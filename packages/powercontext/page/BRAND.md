# PowerContext brand assets

`powercontext-favicon-color.svg` and `powercontext-favicon-reverse.svg` are
unmodified PowerContext Dashboard brand assets from the official repository:

- https://github.com/oceanbase/powercontext/blob/master/src/powercontext/server/dashboard/static/brand/favicon-color.svg

- https://github.com/oceanbase/powercontext/blob/master/src/powercontext/server/dashboard/static/brand/favicon-reverse.svg

The color asset is used on light surfaces; the reverse asset is used when the
official Magpie theme bridge selects dark mode. Neither asset is recolored.

They identify the connected PowerContext service. PowerContext is maintained by
OceanBase; its name and logo belong to their respective owners.
